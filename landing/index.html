Engineered for Better Results

Equipped with state-of-the-art tools designed specifically for cooperative fitness instruction.

Dual-Role Architecture

Log in as either a Coach or a Student. Customize your dashboard experience and access specific tools tailored to your fitness objective.

Activity Dashboard

Coaches can push workouts directly to their trainees, while students view, perform, and check off assigned tasks on completion.

Live Challenges

Compete together! Engage in leaderboard challenges with real-time score updates powered securely by Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Visual Progress Reports

Track performance over a rolling 4-week window using clean bar charts. Pinpoint precisely when and where targets are met.

Tailored Workflows

QuickFit molds itself to fit the exact role you play in the gym environment.

STUDENT

Own Your Training

  • Log completions & submit notes to your Coach
  • Track workout volume and frequency charts
  • Join live challenges and claim leaderboard rank
COACH
